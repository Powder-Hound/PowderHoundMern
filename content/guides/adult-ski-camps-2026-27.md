# Adult Ski Camps 2026/27

*A PowAlert report. Checked September 27, 2026.*

## What an adult camp is, and why it is worth the money

An adult ski camp is a multi-day program where you ski with the same coach and a small group of similar-ability skiers for two to seven days. Most run three or four days. The format is different from a string of one-off lessons: the coach sees your skiing on day one, sets a plan, and builds on it each day, often with video review in between.

For experienced skiers, that continuity is the main value. A single lesson rarely gets past a warm-up and one idea. A camp has time to change a habit, test the change on harder terrain, and confirm it holds when you are tired. Many camps also include access to terrain or coaches you would not find on your own: resident steep-skiing guides, retired World Cup racers, or dedicated women's and 60-plus programs.

This directory lists the camps we could check for 2026/27, with dates, prices, levels and a link to each provider's page.

## How to read this directory

- **Confirmed**: 2026/27 dates and price are both posted on the provider's page.
- **Price TBA**: 2026/27 dates are posted but the price is not.
- **TBA**: the program exists but 2026/27 dates are not posted. Where useful, we show the most recent published figure, labeled with its season.
- **Dates/price TBA, check provider.**: the provider lists the program, but its own page does not confirm 2026/27 dates and price. Any date shown next to this label is from the provider's page.
- Prices are per person, before tax, in the currency shown. Lift access is **not** included unless we say so.

## How to pick a camp

### 1. Rate your level honestly

Most programs use a numbered scale: NASTC takes levels 7–10, and Revelstoke and Extremely Canadian take levels 5–6. Most also give a practical test. Extremely Canadian's is simple: if you can ski an ungroomed black run at your home hill, you belong. If you fall between two levels, book the lower one and tell the school. Most camps regroup after the first morning.

### 2. Match the focus to the goal

- Want to ski steeper terrain? Choose a steep or freeride camp with guides who know the local terrain.
- Want better mechanics? Choose a technique, biomechanics or carving camp.
- Want to ski longer and with less fatigue? The age-specific programs set the pace to suit: Jackson Hole's Masters camp (60+) and Mammoth's Ripping Over 50.
- Women's programs are the largest category in this directory, and several sessions are already sold out.

### 3. Check group size and coach ratio

Published caps in this directory run from four skiers per guide (Steep Skiing Camps, Warren Smith) to eight (Deer Valley women's). Jackson Hole's Lady Shred and Aspen Snowmass cap groups at five. NASTC and Rippin Chix quote 6:1 or better. Under five is excellent. Above eight, you are paying for a guided tour with some tips.

### 4. Know what is included

In North America, lift access is usually not included. Telluride and Mad River Glen list a price with lift; NASTC's Big Sky package includes it. Video is common but not universal. Lunch is included at Jackson Hole, Palisades, Sugarbush and Mad River Glen's Saturday clinic. Lodging is included mainly in the Alta lodge camps, the NASTC packages and the international trips.

### 5. Choose your timing

- **Early season (December)**: prices are lower, for example Taos's US$220 Early Season Ski Weeks. Terrain may be limited; Alta says so in its December camp listing.
- **Mid-January to February**: the most reliable snow cover for steep camps.
- **Spring (March–April)**: longer days and softer snow, but some risk. Palisades cancelled an April 2026 camp for warm temperatures and low snow.

### 6. Work out the cost per instruction hour

These figures are approximate, calculated from published schedules. Where lunch falls inside the posted hours, it is included.

| Program | Approx. hours | Price | Per hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Killington Women of the Mountains | 25 | US$479 | ~US$19 |
| Revelstoke Freeride Camp | 18 | CA$379 | ~CA$21 |
| Telluride Women's Weekend | 20 | US$1,000 | ~US$50 |
| Deer Valley Women's 3-Day | 21 | US$1,100 | ~US$52 |
| Mad River Glen 2-hr private | 2 | US$209 | ~US$105 |

Group camps nearly always cost less per hour than private lessons. Legend-led camps sit at the top of the range; the Ligety camp is about US$1,660 per day.

### 7. Read the cancellation terms before you pay

Terms vary widely:

- **Palisades**: full refund up to seven days out.
- **Deer Valley women's program**: full refund up to two days out.
- **Extremely Canadian**: 100% refund up to 48 hours out, 50% after that.
- **Deer Valley's Ligety and Vogtli clinics**: no refunds at all.
- **Snow conditions**: ask each provider what happens if snow is thin, whether that means a refund, a credit, or a technique-focused day, and get the answer in writing before you pay.

Trip insurance is worth pricing for anything over about US$2,000.

### Questions to ask the provider

- What is the maximum group size, and what happens if my group runs short?
- Will I have the same coach every day?
- Is video analysis included, and do I keep the footage?
- What exactly is included: lift ticket, lunch, lodging, demo skis?
- How are groups formed, and can I move if the fit is wrong?
- What happens if the resort closes terrain or a lift during camp?
- What is the refund policy, and does it differ for medical cancellation?
- For steep or backcountry camps: what avalanche gear is required, and is it provided?
